Features

Built for Financial Analysts

Everything you need to quickly digest financial news without losing critical detail.

Paste Any Article

Works with any financial news source — Reuters, Bloomberg, WSJ, CNBC, and more. Simply copy and paste the full article text.

  • No account required
  • Handles long articles
  • Instant processing

AI-Powered Summary

Powered by Facebook's BART-large-CNN model, fine-tuned on the CNN/DailyMail dataset to produce human-quality abstractive summaries.

  • BART-large-CNN model
  • Abstractive summarization
  • Context-aware output

Accuracy Metrics

Every summary is evaluated with ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L scores, giving you transparent quality metrics used by NLP researchers.

  • ROUGE-1 unigram recall
  • ROUGE-2 bigram precision
  • ROUGE-L longest match
Process

How It Works

Three simple steps from raw article to polished briefing.

01

Paste Your Article

Copy any financial news article from the web and paste it into the input field. The model accepts articles of any length.

02

AI Summarization

The BART model processes your text using transformer-based encoder-decoder architecture, generating a concise abstractive summary.

03

Get Your Briefing

Receive a clear, concise summary alongside ROUGE-1, ROUGE-2, and ROUGE-L evaluation scores measuring summary quality.
